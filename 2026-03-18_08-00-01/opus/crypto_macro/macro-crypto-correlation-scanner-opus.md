## 1. Federal Reserve Policy and Interest Rate Outlook

### Current Stance

The FOMC meeting concluding today (March 17-18, 2026) is expected to deliver a rate hold at the **3.50%-3.75%** federal funds target range. This follows three consecutive 25bp cuts in Q4 2025, after which the Fed paused in January 2026. The effective federal funds rate sits at 3.64%.

### March Summary of Economic Projections (SEP)

Today's meeting includes the quarterly release of the Summary of Economic Projections, commonly known as the "dot plot." Key expectations heading into the release:

- **December 2025 dot plot:** The median Fed official projected one rate cut in 2026, though four officials anticipated two cuts and three envisioned deeper cuts.
- **March 2026 risks:** The biggest market risk is if the updated dot plot shifts the median projection to zero cuts for 2026, reflecting the inflationary impact of surging oil prices from the Iran conflict.
- **Market pricing:** Futures contracts price a gentle glide lower through late 2026, with the fed funds rate near 3.6% currently and approximately 3.4% by early 2027. Markets see near-zero probability of a cut before September-October 2026.

### Crypto Implications

- **Holding pattern is neutral-to-modestly-bullish** for crypto. The market has already priced in no near-term cuts, so the hold itself should not cause volatility.
- **The dot plot is the key event risk.** A hawkish shift (fewer projected cuts) would pressure risk assets including crypto. A dovish surprise (more projected cuts) could catalyze a rally.
- **Chair Powell's press conference** will be parsed for commentary on oil-driven inflation versus growth risks. Any hint of stagflationary concerns could temporarily weigh on crypto, while emphasis on economic resilience and eventual easing would be supportive.
- **Correlation signal:** In the current rate regime (3.5%-3.75%), crypto has historically traded as a risk-on asset. Each 25bp cut in late 2025 corresponded with a 3-5% BTC rally in the 48 hours following the announcement.

## 4. DXY (US Dollar Index) Impact

### Current DXY Status

The US Dollar Index traded at **99.50** on March 18, 2026, sitting just below the psychologically significant 100.00 level after pulling back from a recent 10-month high.

### DXY-Crypto Inverse Correlation

The DXY maintains a well-documented inverse correlation with Bitcoin, typically ranging from **-0.5 to -0.8** over rolling 90-day periods. Key dynamics:

- **DXY above 100:** Generally bearish for crypto, signaling dollar strength and tighter global liquidity conditions that suppress risk asset prices.
- **DXY below 100:** Generally bullish for crypto, indicating loosening dollar conditions and improved global liquidity.
- **Sharp DXY drops (greater than 1% per week):** Historically produce outsized positive moves in BTC.

### Current Macro Drivers of the Dollar

1. **Fed policy (neutral/bullish DXY):** Rate hold supports dollar relative to currencies in easing cycles.
2. **Iran conflict (mixed):** Geopolitical uncertainty typically supports the dollar as a safe haven, but the associated oil shock threatens US economic growth, which is bearish for the dollar.
3. **Current balance:** The DXY's position just below 100 reflects these competing forces. A sustained break below 99 would be strongly bullish for crypto; a move above 101 would be a headwind.

### Crypto Implications

- **The DXY at 99.50 is in a neutral zone** for crypto. It is not providing a strong tailwind (as a sub-97 reading would) nor a significant headwind (as a sustained above-102 reading would).
- **Watch for the FOMC impact on DXY today.** A dovish dot plot could push DXY below 99, creating a bullish impulse for crypto. A hawkish surprise could send DXY above 100.50, pressuring digital assets.
- **Global dollar liquidity** remains the most reliable macro indicator for crypto market direction. Falling DXY typically coincides with expanding global M2 money supply, which is the single strongest macro predictor of crypto bull markets.
