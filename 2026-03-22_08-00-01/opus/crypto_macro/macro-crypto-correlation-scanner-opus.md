## 1. Federal Reserve Policy

### Latest FOMC Decision (March 18, 2026)

The Federal Open Market Committee voted to hold the federal funds rate at **3.50-3.75%**, marking the second consecutive meeting with no change. This was widely expected, with CME FedWatch showing 92%+ probability of a hold heading into the decision.

### Key Takeaways from the Statement

- **Economic activity** continues to expand at a "solid pace," but the Committee noted that job gains have remained low and unemployment is little changed.
- **Inflation remains "somewhat elevated"** -- a wording choice that signals the Fed is not yet confident that price pressures are durably resolved.
- The updated **dot plot** projects only **one rate cut in 2026** and one additional cut in 2027. This represents a meaningful downshift from earlier expectations of two or three cuts this year.
- **Uncertainty** was a dominant theme, with the statement citing geopolitical risks, rising oil prices, and persistent services inflation as key concerns.

### Crypto Market Impact

Bitcoin retreated to the $70,000 threshold following the FOMC announcement, primarily in response to the Fed's elevated inflation forecast and hawkish dot plot. Historically, Bitcoin has rallied after only 1 out of 8 FOMC meetings in 2025, even during an active cutting cycle -- suggesting that the relationship between rate decisions and crypto price action is more nuanced than simple "cuts = bullish" narratives imply.

The current rate environment (3.50-3.75%) still represents a meaningful reduction from the 2023-2024 peak of 5.25-5.50%, but the pace of further easing has slowed dramatically. For crypto, this means:

- **Risk-free rates remain attractive** relative to yield-bearing crypto strategies, limiting capital rotation into DeFi.
- **Liquidity expansion has stalled**, removing a key tailwind for speculative assets.
- **Forward guidance matters more than the decision itself** -- the shift from two projected cuts to one was the primary driver of the post-FOMC selloff.

## 4. DXY (US Dollar Index) Impact

### Current DXY Status

- **DXY Level (March 20):** 99.50, up 0.27% on the session
- **Trend:** The dollar has strengthened back toward the psychologically significant 100 level after touching a 4-month low earlier in the year.
- **90-day BTC-DXY correlation:** Approximately -0.5 to -0.8 (inverse relationship)

### Dollar-Crypto Inverse Relationship

The DXY and Bitcoin maintain a well-documented **inverse correlation**. When the dollar strengthens, it typically signals:

- Higher real yields making dollar-denominated assets more attractive
- Risk aversion as capital flows to "safe haven" currencies
- Tighter global liquidity conditions, particularly for emerging markets that denominate debt in USD

**Current dynamics:**

- DXY rising above 100 creates a significant headwind for Bitcoin attempting to reclaim $80,000.
- The dollar's strength is being driven by (a) the Fed's relatively hawkish stance compared to other central banks, (b) geopolitical safe-haven flows, and (c) persistent yield differentials favoring USD.
- A reversal is possible: if DXY drops below 96.2 (a key technical level), analysts expect a bullish catalyst for crypto around April-May 2026.

### Impact Channels

| DXY Movement | Crypto Impact | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| DXY above 100 | Bearish | Capital flows to dollar, reduces appetite for risk assets |
| DXY at 96-100 | Neutral | Mixed signals, crypto trades on idiosyncratic factors |
| DXY below 96 | Bullish | Dollar weakness drives rotation into alternative stores of value |

The current DXY level near 99.50 places crypto in a **mildly bearish-to-neutral** zone from a dollar perspective. The key level to watch is 100: a sustained break above could push Bitcoin toward the $65,000 support, while a rejection and retreat toward 96 could catalyze a rally toward $80,000+.
