3. **Lower-than-expected CPI** does the reverse, signaling potential easing and boosting risk appetite.

### Bond-Crypto Transmission

The mechanism linking rising bond yields to crypto weakness operates through several channels:

1. **Opportunity cost:** Higher risk-free yields make non-yielding assets like Bitcoin less attractive relative to Treasuries.
2. **Discount rate effect:** Rising yields increase the discount rate applied to future cash flows, compressing valuations of growth assets and risk assets broadly.
3. **DeFi yield compression:** On-chain DeFi yields must compete with 4.4% risk-free Treasury rates, reducing the incremental attractiveness of yield farming.
4. **Stablecoin dynamics:** Higher Treasury yields increase returns for stablecoin issuers (who hold Treasuries as reserves) but can attract capital away from on-chain deployment into Treasury-backed products.

### Stagflation Risk Assessment

The most dangerous scenario for crypto is **stagflation** -- simultaneous rising inflation and slowing growth. The Iran-driven oil shock is pushing inflation expectations higher while threatening GDP growth through elevated energy costs. In a stagflationary regime, the Fed cannot cut rates (due to inflation), removing the primary catalyst for crypto rallies, while economic weakness suppresses risk appetite. This scenario is now being actively discussed by Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, and other major institutions.
