## Market Context

The crypto market experienced a sharp sell-off during the week of March 24-28, 2026. Bitcoin dropped from $71,000 to $66,457 before recovering slightly to ~$66,871. The crash was driven by three converging factors:

1. **Geopolitical escalation:** Iran threatened to block a second global oil chokepoint, pushing oil above $100 and triggering a risk-off rotation
2. **Massive options expiry:** The largest crypto options expiry of 2026 ($14B) triggered $450M in liquidations, wiping out 122,000+ traders
3. **Macro headwinds:** 10-year Treasury yield near 4.5%, dollar index up 0.57%, and persistent 15% global tariff overhang

Bitcoin is down ~20% from its 52-week high of $126,079. Bitcoin dominance sits at 58.16%. Whale wallets holding 1,000+ BTC have reached a record 2,140 addresses, suggesting smart money is accumulating.

## Risk Management Rules

1. **Never invest more than you can afford to lose** — crypto remains highly volatile
2. **Use stop losses** — all plays above include defined stop loss levels
3. **Dollar-cost average (DCA)** — in extreme fear conditions, spread entries over 2-4 weeks
4. **Position sizing** — no single altcoin position should exceed 10% of portfolio
5. **Take profits incrementally** — sell 33% at TP1, 33% at TP2, let remainder ride to TP3
6. **Monitor macro** — Fed policy shifts and DXY movements can override technicals
