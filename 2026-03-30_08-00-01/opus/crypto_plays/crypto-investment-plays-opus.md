## Market Overview

The crypto market is in a significant correction phase as of late March 2026. Bitcoin has dropped nearly 50% from its late-2025 all-time high and trades near $66,500. Ethereum broke below $2,000 for the first time since mid-2024, down 60% from its August 2025 high of $4,953. Solana is down 72% from its cycle high. The Iran-Israel conflict and Red Sea shipping threats have accelerated selling pressure, with the market shedding over $80 billion since March 24 alone.

However, on-chain metrics signal an accumulation phase: large BTC holders added approximately 270,000 BTC during this consolidation. Spot Bitcoin ETFs hold a combined $130 billion in AUM. Bitcoin's weekly RSI is deeply oversold -- a condition that has historically preceded sharp recoveries. BTC dominance is rising above 55%, indicating capital rotation from altcoins into Bitcoin, which typically reverses once BTC stabilizes.

**Key macro thesis:** This is a high-conviction accumulation window for established large-cap assets. Risk management is critical given ongoing geopolitical uncertainty.
